Privacy Policy

Star Outpost Tower Defense
Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies on the collection, use and disclosure of information when you use the Star Outpost Tower Defense mobile game.

1. Information Collection

We do NOT collect, store, share or transmit any personally identifiable information from users.
This is a standalone offline tower defense game. The game runs locally on your Android device. No account login is required. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device ID or any other personal data.

2. Game Data

All game progress, settings and in-game values are saved locally on your device only. This save data never leaves your phone and is not uploaded to any server.

3. Third-Party Services

If we integrate third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) in future versions of the game:
- AdMob may collect anonymous non-personal data for advertising optimization, subject to Google’s own Privacy Policy.
- You can review Google AdMob privacy information here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In the current version, no third-party data collection service is integrated.

4. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years old. If you believe we have collected information of a child under 13, please contact us immediately.
